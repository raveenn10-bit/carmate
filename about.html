OUR STORY

Driven by automotive culture in the South.

Based at 197 Hotel Suniru Lanka in Makuluwa, Galle, Carmate Modifications has grown into one of the South's most trusted destinations for automotive styling, custom bodywork, and lighting upgrades.

Whether enhancing performance, elevating road presence, or customizing your ride to reflect your individual taste, we treat every single car that enters our workshop like a flagship show build.

Our team provides detailed consultations in both English and Sinhala, ensuring complete clarity on materials, fitment tolerances, wiring safety, and color matching before any tool touches your car.

OUR COMMITMENT

The Carmate Standards

Principles that guide every bumper fitment, wiring harness, and polish cycle.

01

Millimeter Precision

We do not settle for approximate gaps or misaligned seams. Every kit is dry-fitted, trimmed, adjusted, and re-checked until it looks OEM+.

02

Wiring & Safety Integrity

All electrical work for DRLs, projectors, and ambient systems utilizes insulated waterproof harnesses, proper fuses, and relay protection.

03

Community & Trust

With 1,200+ followers across Sri Lanka and a 5-star reputation in Galle, our work speaks for itself through long-lasting client relationships.
